SINDy-RL on the Cart-Pole Swing-Up: Reproduction, Diagnostics and a Learned-Model Simulator

An independent, from-scratch reproduction of SINDy-RL (Zolman et al., 2025): a Dyna-style loop that learns a sparse, symbolic dynamics model (E-SINDy), trains PPO inside it, and refits it from a small amount of real data. Reproduction and analysis by Humain.

1. Method: the SINDy-RL loop

Instead of learning from millions of real steps, PPO trains almost entirely inside the cheap symbolic surrogate. After every 40 PPO updates the policy is rolled out once in the real environment, the new transitions are added to the data, and Ξ is refit. The model stays a short list of readable equations.

2. Learned dynamics (final model, update 39)

x⁺      =  0.99994·x + 0.01·ẋ
cos θ⁺  =  0.99975·cos θ − 0.01·sin θ·θ̇
sin θ⁺  =  0.99910·sin θ + 0.01·cos θ·θ̇
ẋ⁺      =  0.99927·ẋ − 0.01117·cos θ·sin θ + u·(0.0581 + 0.03894·cos²θ + 0.01918·sin²θ)
θ̇⁺      =  0.99951·θ̇ + 0.14807·sin θ − 0.13537·cos θ·u

14 non-zero coefficients out of 205 possible. The structure is physically meaningful: Euler-like position updates, a gravity term (0.148·sin θ, the unstable upright point), and a control gain proportional to cos θ that flips sign between top and bottom.

4. Results: real return per dynamics update

Each point is the deterministic policy evaluated in the real environment after one more round of 40 PPO updates and one model refit (single seed). The shaded band is the plateau I read off the paper’s Fig. 3 for the quadratic SINDy-RL variant at a similar real-data budget; treat it as indicative, since the paper reports a 20-seed median. Returns fall during the first updates, while PPO optimises an early, data-poor model, then recover once on-policy data arrives.

Two of my own early hypotheses (control-gain identifiability at the top, rail contact) were refuted by the diagnostics I wrote. That narrowed the problem to the PPO configuration rather than the dictionary model.

6. What I built and learned

  • Full reproduction from scratch: custom surrogate Gym environment, E-SINDy and STLRidge solvers, a 41-term control-affine library, the Dyna-style training loop, real-environment evaluation and data collection.
  • Diagnostic toolkit: scripts that compare surrogate and real one-step gains, replay real action sequences through the surrogate, check the real cart against the rail, and inspect RLlib checkpoints.
  • Interpretability in practice: the final controller model is a 14-term equation set that I could verify term by term against physics and against the original paper.
  • Skill takeaways: sparse regression and system identification, closed-loop identifiability, PPO internals (critic clipping, schedules), reproducibility under version constraints (Ray 2.6.3, torch 2.0.1).
